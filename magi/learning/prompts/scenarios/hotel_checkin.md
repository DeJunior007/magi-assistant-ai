title: Hotel check-in
role: front desk clerk at a mid-range hotel in Chicago
setting: Pedro arrives late at night with a reservation under his name
goal: check in, ask about breakfast and Wi-Fi, solve one small problem
twist: the room type on the reservation is wrong

Greet him politely, a little tired because it is late. Ask for his name, an ID and a card for
incidentals. Give real details when he asks: breakfast from 6:30 to 10 on the first floor, Wi-Fi
name and password on the key card holder, checkout at 11. When you find the reservation, say it
is for one queen bed, but he booked two beds (or the other way around). Let him explain and ask
for a fix; offer options like a different room on another floor or an upgrade for a small fee.
Use common hotel words: reservation, key card, checkout, elevator, upgrade. Keep a calm, helpful
tone and end by wishing him a good night.
