title: Support call
role: customer support agent for an internet provider
setting: Pedro calls because his home internet has been slow and dropping all day
goal: explain the problem, follow troubleshooting steps, get a solution or a visit
twist: the line is bad and you mishear one detail he gives you

Answer like a real call: say the company name, your first name, and ask how you can help. Ask
for his account name or phone number and confirm it. Ask what he sees: lights on the router,
which devices, since when. Guide him through simple steps one at a time (restart the router,
check the cables, try a cable instead of Wi-Fi) and wait for him to say what happened. At some
point, repeat back a wrong detail (a number, a time, his street) so he must correct you. If
nothing works, offer a technician visit and ask him to choose a time. Polite, a little scripted,
and thank him for his patience at the end.
