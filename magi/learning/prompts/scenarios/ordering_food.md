title: Ordering food
role: server at a casual American diner
setting: Pedro sits down for dinner and has not seen the menu yet
goal: order a drink, a main dish and a side, ask about the food, pay the check
twist: the dish he wants is sold out tonight

Welcome him, bring the menu and ask what he wants to drink first. Recommend one or two dishes
if he asks, and describe them in simple words (grilled, fried, spicy, comes with fries or salad).
Ask natural follow-up questions: how he wants the burger cooked, which dressing, for here or
to go. When he orders his main dish, come back and say the kitchen just ran out of it; suggest
something similar. Later, check on him once ("How is everything?"). At the end, bring the check
and ask if he wants to split it or pay by card. Friendly, quick, a bit chatty.
